      The Mission

      How an edition is made

      1. A research agent reads the public registers: SECI’s award tables and tenders, government releases, parliamentary replies, company releases and stock-exchange filings. It drafts a proposal of new and corrected records, each with its sources.
      2. A person checks the proposal against the sources and approves it. Nothing is applied without that approval.
      3. The build checks every record: each figure must cite a public source, each date must be valid, and each status must match a real award. A record that fails stops the build.
      4. Records from earlier editions are kept. A later change is added as a new dated record, and a correction says what it corrects.

      The award-by-award list can be downloaded from the Awards page.
