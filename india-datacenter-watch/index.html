How to read this dashboard — the five rules behind the numbers

Two load layers, one buildout

Filed grid connectivity (the Grid Access tab) is what a datacenter has formally asked the grid for — MW quantum + a start date, filed with a transmission utility. It covers the whole site (cooling and growth headroom included), so it can exceed the press-reported IT MW for the same project. Tracked IT load is what press and company statements disclose. Filings are the authoritative layer; press is the enrichment layer.

Speculative MW never leaks into headlines

MoU-stage projects are shown but excluded from headline counts until land, financing, a grid connection, or construction evidence appears. IT-load MW is recorded only when a source discloses it — nothing is imputed.

Clean-power claims sit on a firmness ladder

A corporate renewable pledge is not a site power plan. Every project is placed on a ladder from “undisclosed” up to hourly clean-firm supply, and site power is never inferred from a group-level claim.

Provenance is tiered and enforced

Every source is tagged ✓ Primary (government / regulator instrument), official (company), or media. Policy items, connectivity filings, and grid denominators must cite a primary source — the build refuses silence on violations, and cited documents are archived locally for audit.

Dates and history say what they are

Trend history is reconstructed from first-report dates until real monthly snapshots accumulate — it is labelled as such. Power-flow dates from filings are marked declared or estimated; estimated dates render dashed. Stale records (not re-verified in 60+ days) carry an amber dot.

Pipeline by stage — firm vs. speculative

Known IT-load MW by project stage. MoU rows are shown but held out of the firm headline until land, financing, grid connection, or construction evidence appears.

Authoritative load · grid connectivity

Grid Access

The connectivity / GNA register — what each datacenter has actually asked the grid for: the MW quantum and the start date of power flow, filed with the transmission utility. CTUIL handles inter-state (ISTS) connectivity; the largest loads (like Lodha's ~3 GW) connect intra-state via a state utility. This is the dashboard's most authoritative load signal — a regulatory filing, not a press release.

Press vs filings — the reconciliation bridge

The same buildout measured two ways. Press-tracked IT MW is what companies disclose; filed connectivity quantum is what they asked the grid for (whole-site draw incl. cooling and headroom). The gap is the story: filings run ahead of disclosures.
