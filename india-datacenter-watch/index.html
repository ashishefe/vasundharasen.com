            Filed and announced load measure different things

            A grid filing is what a developer has formally asked the transmission utility for. It covers the whole site, cooling and growth room included. Announced IT load is what press and company statements disclose about the servers alone. The two do not add up and should not be compared site by site as like for like.

            Where the figures come from

            Every figure here comes from grid registers, regulator orders, company statements or press. Each site’s sources are linked under its name. We do not estimate capacity from satellite images.
